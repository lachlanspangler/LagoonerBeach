Lagooner Beach — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that phone home. The only data that ever leaves your device is for the optional leaderboards, and only after you choose a name and post a score.

What stays on your device

Your progress (best scores, settings, daily streaks, reefs cleared, your Beach Journal and your leaderboard name) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data) deletes it.

Leaderboards (optional)

Nothing is sent until you tap Post on a leaderboard name screen. A posted score sends:

  • Public: the name you chose (1–7 letters A–Z), your score and number of moves, and a creature from your Beach Journal as your avatar.
  • Not public: the moves of that game and its seed (the server replays them to check the score is real), a random player id created on your device the first time you post, and the app version.

The random id is not a device or advertising id. Names can't contain digits, spaces or symbols, so they can't carry contact details, and they pass a word filter. After your first post, later Daily and Classic results are posted automatically under the same name; Erase all progress stops this. When online, the game also asks the server for a seed for your next Classic game so it can be ranked; before your first post that request carries no identifier. At that game's first move, the game tells the server it has started (sending only the signed seed ticket), so the server can tell how long it took. Builds without an online leaderboard show on-device "Demo" boards and send nothing.

Where it's stored, and for how long

The leaderboard runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1) on our behalf. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver each request; we don't store it. To limit abuse we keep a salted one-way hash of the address (never the address) as a rate-limit counter, and next to a salted hash of your player id (never the id) to limit how many players one network can add. Deletion runs hourly, so each item is gone within an hour of its limit: daily boards 30 days, weekly boards 8 weeks, the all-time board until you delete it; used seeds and game start times 72 hours; rate-limit counters 1 hour; per-network counters 24 hours (for a Daily board, until that Daily closes, at most 52 hours after it opens). Backups roll off within 30 days. We don't sell or share this data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase all progress deletes everything on your device and asks the server to delete your player, name and scores (sent at the next launch if you're offline). You can also email us to ask.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a Daily, the game hands a short text (date, score, streak, a small emoji picture) to your device's share sheet. The game doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

Suitable for all ages. Leaderboards are optional; names are 7 letters A–Z and filtered; there is no chat or messaging, and the player id is random and only used to run the boards.

Links to charities

The About page links to coastal-wildlife charities. Those links open the charity's own website in your browser, with no referrer and nothing added to the address; we don't record taps and never handle donations.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before that version ships, and anything involving data will be opt-in.
