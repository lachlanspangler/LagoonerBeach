Lagooner Beach — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Lagooner Beach collects no personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporters, no third-party SDKs that phone home, and no network requests while you play.

What stays on your device

Your progress — best scores, settings, daily streaks, reefs cleared, and your Beach Journal — is saved only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data) deletes it.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a Daily, the game hands a short text (date, score, streak, a small emoji picture) to your device's share sheet. The game doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

Suitable for all ages; collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example optional ads or purchases), this page will be updated before that version ships, and anything involving data will be opt-in.
