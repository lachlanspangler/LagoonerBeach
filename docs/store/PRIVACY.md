# Lagooner Beach — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

**Lagooner Beach collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising
identifiers, no crash reporters, no third-party SDKs that phone home, and no network requests made by
the game while you play.

## What stays on your device
Your progress is saved **only on your device** (browser local storage, or the app's private
storage on iOS/Android): best scores, settings (sound, music, haptics, theme, style), daily streaks,
reef puzzles cleared, and your Beach Journal. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app (or clearing the
site's data) deletes it.

## Sharing
If you tap **Share** after a Daily, the game hands a short text (date, score, streak, a small emoji
picture) to your device's share sheet. Where it goes is up to you; the game doesn't send it anywhere
itself.

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this ever changes (for example, optional ads or purchases), this policy will be updated **before**
that version ships, and anything involving data will be opt-in.
